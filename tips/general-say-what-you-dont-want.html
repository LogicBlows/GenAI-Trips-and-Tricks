Say what you don’t want

8 Oct 2026 · General · Prompting

Models default to helpful, verbose, and generic. A short don’t list often saves more time than adding another paragraph of context.

Example — internal doc

Add to any prompt:

Don’t: mention competitors by name, suggest external SaaS we haven’t approved, or commit/push without me asking.
1. Goal in one line

What “done” looks like.

2. Don’t list (2–4 bullets)

Scope, tone, tools, or actions to avoid.

3. Reuse in Projects / rules

ChatGPT Project instructions or Cursor rules — same constraints every time.

Internal bank use: never paste credentials, customer data, or unapproved third-party links into prompts.
